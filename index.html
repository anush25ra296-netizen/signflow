Model information

Browser-based landmark classifier

SignFlow uses the MIT-licensed ASL Alphabet Classifier model by AmimulBmeIU. Its model card reports 94.15% validation accuracy for static letters A–Y excluding J and Z; real-world results can differ. J and Z require movement-aware recognition and are not supported by this static classifier. Model details and license.
